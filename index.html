Muhammad Zubair — Research Website

Muhammad Zubair is a PhD candidate in the Department of Biomedical Engineering at The Pennsylvania State University. In January 2027, he will join The University of Texas at Austin as a Postdoctoral Fellow in Computer Science.

His research lies at the intersection of wearable bioelectronics, neural interfaces, biomedical signal processing, and artificial intelligence. He develops conformal physiological sensors and computational methods for translating EEG, EOG, EMG, and ECG signals into reliable information for human–machine interfaces and health monitoring.

His work spans epidermal sensing, sleep and drowsiness monitoring, physiological signal decoding, and intelligent assistive systems, with publications in venues including Science Advances, National Science Review, and IEEE journals.
